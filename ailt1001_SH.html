AILT1001 in Shanghai

Artificial Intelligence Literacy I at HKU-CDS Teaching and Research Site

Course Overview

We are excited to announce a special intensive delivery of AILT1001-1SH Artificial Intelligence Literacy I, to be held during Reading Week (Oct 12 to Oct 16, 2026) in Semester One at our HKU-CDS Teaching and Research Site in Shanghai. Lecture and studio content are identical to other subclasses, but condensed into one intensive week. Participants will receive free accommodation and airfare subsidy.

Duration: 5 days (Oct 12 - Oct 16, 2026) + 2 travel days (Oct 11 & Oct 17, 2026)

Location: HKU-CDS Teaching and Research Site, Shanghai

Class Size: 160 students

* Students are required to attend ALL lectures and studios, and complete all required coursework to be eligible for the subsidy.

* Students are expected to check in to the dormitory on Sunday, Oct 11 from 12nn to 5pm (tentative). No late check-in service on the day will be provided.

Course Assessment

Assessment methods are the same as regular AILT1001 subclasses. For more details, please refer to the course home page.

  • Participation (20%): In-class activities
  • Group Project (40%): Mini AI project
  • Final Test (40%): The final test will be held during the final lecture of the semester.

Final Grade Release: Same as the main campus time

Full attendance at lectures and studios is required to be eligible for the subsidy. If you are unable to attend any session due to illness or other valid reasons, please refer to the sick leave policy on the course home page.
